Blackout Lab

The power is out in a quantum research facility, and the lift platforms have become entangled with the laboratory's qubits, exhibiting macroscopic quantum behaviour!

Use your flashlight to perform measurements and terminals to alter quantum states.

Climb the floors to reach the terrace and restore power to the laboratory.

Developed by Dev Chauhan, Yusuf Ahmed, and Ayesha Iqbal

Pause and options

Fall into the gap and you lose a life and the floor resets. Lose all three on one floor and you start again from floor 1. There is no timer. Unlimited lives are in the pause menu.
